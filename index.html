02 / Output settings

Balanced targets under 10 MB for short clips without exceeding its normal bitrate. Longer videos may need more than 10 MB to avoid excessive blur; size cannot be guaranteed by browser hardware encoders.

Detects HLG/PQ/Dolby Vision color metadata where available. SDR conversion uses frame-by-frame color-managed rendering, not real-time screen recording. Browser tone mapping can vary; compare colors before sharing. Never assume HDR is ordinary SDR.

Quality Guard avoids re-encoding already-compatible MP4s. For actual re-encoding, x264 is usually higher-fidelity but slower and experimental in-browser. Hardware/WebCodecs quality varies by device.
