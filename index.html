The new browser-native engine decodes and encodes media frames with timestamps, not a real-time screen recording. H.264 video is encoded around 3.8 Mbps at up to 1080p / 30 FPS; audio is copied when compatible. A separate x264 engine is optional. No engine can guarantee that WhatsApp won't recompress your Status. Media stays on your device; only the open-source processing library is fetched from a CDN. Use short clips first. HEVC/HDR color conversion and some iPhone file types may be unsupported. Re-encoding can reduce quality even when dimensions and bitrate look good.
