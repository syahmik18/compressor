Status HD uses H.264 around 3.4 Mbps with AAC audio as a practical starting point from one PureStatus sample, not a quality guarantee. The single-thread FFmpeg engine runs on your device without SharedArrayBuffer or canvas re-recording. The engine (~30 MB) is downloaded from a CDN when first used; your media is not uploaded. Long videos and iPhone memory limits may still prevent a conversion. Recommended: short clips under 50 MB on phones, under 120 MB on desktop. HDR-to-SDR color conversion is not yet reliable. Source GPS and camera metadata are removed from compressed exports. WhatsApp can still recompress Status videos.
